Write in ink

Plain-text ink with live compile, error highlighting, jump-to-line, and a story bible that feeds an LLM.

See the shape

A knot and stitch map with coverage, dead ends, and unreachable branches. Click a node to jump to its source.

Build packs

Self-contained packs with compiled ink, assets, covers, and themes. Sign a catalogue and publish it anywhere.

Play offline

The PWA and desktop apps install packs, save progress, and work without a connection.
